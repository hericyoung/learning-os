## Connections

| From | Relationship | To | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|

Use these relationship labels consistently:

* `builds-on` — depends on a prior concept.
* `connects-to` — shares a useful structural relationship.
* `taught-in` — is developed in a lesson or course.
* `evidenced-by` — is supported or tested by an observation.
* `challenges` — exposes a limitation or contradiction.
* `distilled-in` — is compressed into a lesson summary.
* `synthesized-in` — is integrated into a milestone.
* `reflected-in` — is reconsidered, revised, or applied in learner reflection.

## Maintenance Check

When adding or updating an artifact:

1. Search this index and the repository for overlapping knowledge.
2. Prefer a canonical existing artifact over a near-duplicate.
3. Add or update its registry entry.
4. Add at least one meaningful relationship unless the artifact is explicitly marked as awaiting connection.
5. Ensure reciprocal navigation is present where useful—for example, a model links to its lesson and the lesson summary links back to the model.
6. Remove or mark stale links when an artifact is superseded.
